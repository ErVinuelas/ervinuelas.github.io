I consider myself passionate about science, especially Mathematics, during my last years of high school/gymnasium I also discovered an interest in Computer Science. This led me to study both the bachelor of Mathematics and Computer Science.

Both my Bachelor's Degree Final Projects are about software verification, [one](https://github.com/ErVinuelas/verification-segments) using the [Dafny](https://dafny.org/) language and the [other](https://github.com/costa-group/termination_coq) using the [Rocq Proof Assistant](https://rocq-prover.org/). This topic is among my preferred areas of discourse but I also enjoy other topics, such as Cryptography or Machine Learning.

That is why I decided to study a Master of Computer Science at Aarhus University, where I focused on the Cryptography and Formal Methods track. I really enjoyed [my time in Denmark](/p/master-thesis/). I recently finished the degree with a thesis on formally verifying a real-world implementation of the AES block cipher using [Hax](https://github.com/hacspec/hax) and [Lean](https://leanprover.github.io/). You can read more about it in [this post](/p/master-thesis/).

In the past I have worked as a student assistant in the AI&ML department of Salling Group. I learnt a lot during my time there and spend most of my time in a project which aimed to reduce the theft in the shops. We used different approaches, but I mainly focused on applying CNNs and other Deep Learning techniques. 

But from April 2025 to July 2026, I worked as a research assistant at Aarhus University under the guidance of [Bas Spitters](https://users-cs.au.dk/spitters/), where I worked on the [SSProve](https://github.com/SSProve/ssprove) framework and formally verified the KZG polynomial commitment scheme using it. Right now I am doing an internship at the [Ethereum Foundation](https://verified-zkevm.org).

On my spare time, I like to practice judo (for the last 17 years!) and recently started to do some climbing. I also enjoy a good book and a tasty meal.
